# Cambridge English Young Learners (YLE) - Official Wordlist Reference
*(Pre A1 Starters, A1 Movers, A2 Flyers - Exam Wordlists from 2018)*

---

## 1. Introduction
These wordlists are designed specifically for young learners aged 6–12 [2]. The exams (Pre A1 Starters, A1 Movers, and A2 Flyers) are designed to make learning fun, colourful, and activity-based, motivating children to learn and earn 'shields' that record their progress [2, 127].
At all levels, candidates need to be able to understand the words on the wordlist for their test [2]. The vocabulary was selected with young learners in mind and focuses on words relevant to young children’s experiences of the world, such as animals, toys, home, school, and leisure activities [155].

### Modals [105]
