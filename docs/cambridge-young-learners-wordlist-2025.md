## 1. Introduction to the 2025 Wordlist
The Cambridge English Young Learners (YLE) exams are designed specifically for children aged 6–12 [371]. These exams are fun, colourful, and activity-based, motivating children to learn and earn 'shields' that record their progress [371]. 

At all levels (Pre A1 Starters, A1 Movers, and A2 Flyers), candidates are expected to understand and use the words specified on the official wordlists for their respective tests [372]. The vocabulary is selected with young learners in mind and focuses on topics relevant to their everyday experiences, such as animals, toys, home, school, and leisure activities [155].

### Grammatical Key:
The following abbreviations are used in the wordlists to indicate parts of speech [373]:

| Abbreviation | Part of Speech | Abbreviation | Part of Speech |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *adj* | adjective | *adv* | adverb |
| *conj* | conjunction | *det* | determiner |
| *dis* | discourse marker | *excl* | exclamation |
| *int* | interrogative | *n* | noun |
| *poss* | possessive | *prep* | preposition |
| *pron* | pronoun | *v* | verb |
